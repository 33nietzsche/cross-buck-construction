Services

Electrical. Operating. Plumbing. Ranch builds. General jobs.

1. Electrical

New circuits, lighting, outlets, shop and barn power, troubleshooting, and property electrical work. Scoped per job — safety first.

2. Operating

Pipeline install, caliche roads, dig and dump. Equipment work for site prep and rural dirt jobs when the place needs more than a shovel.

3. Plumbing

Repairs, replacements, livestock water troughs, shop and house lines, and general plumbing for rural and small-town places.

4. Corrals & livestock handling

Working corrals, pens, alleys, and basic handling layouts for cattle that actually move.

5. Fences & gates

New fence, rebuilds, gates, and line repairs. Materials matched to the ground and the job.

6. Concrete & framing

Pads, footings, flatwork, pours, and structural framing for outbuildings, lean-tos, and additions.

7. General — basically anything you need done

Odds and ends around the property, small tear-outs, load-and-dump runs, “nobody will call me back” jobs. If it’s legal and safe, we’ll tell you if we can take it and what it costs. No mystery.

Service area

Wolfforth to Brownfield to Lamesa, and the small towns and ranch roads in between.

Outside that stretch? Ask anyway — sometimes the job still works.
