Frequently asked questions

What kind of odd jobs do you take?

Cross Buck Construction takes odds and ends around the property: small tear-outs, load-and-dump runs, gate and step fixes, and the jobs nobody else calls back about. If it is legal, safe and we can scope it, we will tell you straight whether we can take it and what it costs.

Do you haul off junk and debris?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction loads and hauls off junk, debris, brush and tear-out material from properties across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. We scope the load, access and dump fees up front, so the quote shows the real cost. Send photos and your town to (806) 496-6999.

Can you tear out an old shed, pen or fence?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction tears out small old structures such as sheds, pens and fence, then hauls the material off. If you plan to build back, we can also do the dirt work, concrete, framing or new fence, so the tear-out and the rebuild stay with one outfit.

Can I send a list of different jobs?

Yes. Send the whole list with photos and your town to (806) 496-6999 or through the contact form. Cross Buck Construction covers nine trades, so a mixed list of dirt, steel, concrete, fence and water work can often be handled in one trip instead of several contractors.
