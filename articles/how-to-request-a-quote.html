How to request a quote from Cross Buck Construction

Call, text, email, or the form. Photos help.

Short answer: Call or text 806-496-6999, email crossbuckconstruction@gmail.com, or use the quote form. Photos help. Name, phone, email, town, job type, and what you need are the form fields.

Three steps: talk it through, straight quote (what is included, what is not, when we can start), then get it done. That is a clear scope, not a written warranty text.

Checklist before you send

  • Town
  • Job type: Electrical; Solar well install or house array; Welding; Operating (caliche / dig & dump); Ranch and farm plumbing / pipeline; Corral / pens; Fence / gates; Concrete; Framing; or General / mix / not sure
  • Photos of the job, attached in the email that opens
  • What you need in plain language
  • Photos of the area if you have them
  • Access notes (locked gate, livestock, power off)

You deal with the person who shows up. If it is not legal or safe, or we cannot take it, we will tell you straight.
