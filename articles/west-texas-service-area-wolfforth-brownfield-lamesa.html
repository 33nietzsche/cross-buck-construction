Does Cross Buck Construction work in my West Texas town?

Most of West Texas · Lubbock to Midland and Odessa · Wolfforth, Brownfield, Lamesa and surrounding communities

Short answer: We cover most of West Texas, from Lubbock to Midland and Odessa, including Wolfforth, Brownfield, Lamesa and surrounding communities. If you are not sure you are in range, say your town when you call or write — we will tell you yes or no without the runaround.

That is most of West Texas, not all of Texas and not a claim that every town is automatically covered. Town confirmation stays useful. Ask if you are unsure.

What to send when you ask

  • Town or area
  • What you need (electrical, operating, plumbing, corral, fence, concrete, framing, or mix)
  • Photos if you have them
