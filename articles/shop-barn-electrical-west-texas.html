Shop and barn electrical: what Cross Buck takes on

Shops, barns, houses — scoped per job, safety first

Short answer: Zach is a licensed electrician. Cross Buck Construction does electrical work from service upgrades and new installations to motors and pumps — plus shops, barns, houses, circuits, lighting, outlets, and troubleshooting. Jobs are scoped per job, with safety first.

Call or send photos of the work you need. We will tell you what is in scope before we start.

What to tell us

Talk it through (call, text, or photos), get a straight quote of what is included and what is not, then the work.

  1. Town
  2. Shop, barn, house, or mixed
  3. What fails or what you want added
  4. Photos of the panel area and the space if you can take them safely — do not open energized equipment yourself

If the job is not legal or safe to take, we will tell you whether we can take it.
