Frequently asked questions

What fence and gate work does Cross Buck Construction do?

Cross Buck Construction is a licensed electrical shop that also does general contracting, including fences and gates, and emergency repairs. On fence work we build new fence lines, rebuild old ones, hang gates and repair lines, with materials matched to the ground and the job. That includes boundary fence, cross fence and livestock fence across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico.

Should I repair my fence or replace the whole line?

It depends on the condition of the line, and sometimes rebuilding one stretch beats replacing the whole thing. Cross Buck Construction looks at washouts, leaning posts, broken braces and loose wire, then tells you which makes sense. Photos of the line and corners help.

Do you build and hang gates?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds pipe gates and decorative entry gates in our own welding work, then hangs them to swing and latch true. We also fix gates that sag or drag.

Why do fence corners and gates need braces?

Braces take the pull of the wire so corner and gate posts do not lean and the line does not sag. H-braces and X-braces are used at corners and gates. The X-brace, called a cross buck, is where Cross Buck Construction gets its name.

How much does fence cost per foot?

There is no flat per-foot price, because cost depends on the fence type, the ground, the number of corners, braces and gates, and access along the line. Send your town, the rough length and photos, and call or text (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.

Do you build corrals and pens as well as fence?

Yes. Corrals and pens are a separate service at Cross Buck Construction, alongside fences and gates. If you need both a pen and a boundary fence, say so when you ask for a quote and both can be scoped together.
