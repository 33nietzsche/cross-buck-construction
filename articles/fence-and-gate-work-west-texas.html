Fence and gate work on West Texas places

Posts true, line tight

Short answer: Cross Buck Construction lists fences and gates as a service: fence lines, gates, rebuilds, and repairs. Materials are matched to the ground and the job. The name Cross Buck is the X-brace on ranch gates and fences.

No per-foot price is published on this site. A quote is scoped to your line.

What to include

  • Town / area
  • New line, rebuild, gate only, or repair
  • Rough length and what is already in the ground, if you know
  • Photos of the line, corners, and any washouts or leaning posts
  • Whether livestock will be on the fence

Corrals and pens are a separate listed service. If you need both a pen and a boundary fence, say so.
