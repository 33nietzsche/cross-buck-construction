Drywall, doors and trim

Who handles rental make-ready and turnover repairs in Lubbock?

Cross Buck Construction takes rental make-ready and turnover repair inquiries in Lubbock and Wolfforth. Property managers can send one room-by-room list for small repairs such as drywall patches, sticking doors, loose trim and small flooring fixes.

Mark what must be fixed before the next tenant moves in. Flag damp walls or signs of an active leak before asking for patching or paint. If you need photos of the finished repairs, include that in the requested scope.

To get a quote, send your rental repair list with the property’s town, the target move-in date and when the unit will be available. You can text repair photos without tenant details or access codes. We’ll confirm the work and availability before scheduling.
