Frequently asked questions

Do you pour concrete pads and slabs?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction pours concrete pads and slabs for shops, equipment, troughs and outbuildings, plus footings and flatwork. We can also handle the dirt work to clear and level the site first, so prep and pour are one job. Call or text (806) 496-6999 with your town.

Do you frame sheds, lean-tos and additions?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction does structural framing for sheds, lean-tos, outbuildings, additions and ranch buildings across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. Tell us the size, what the building is for and send any plans or photos, and we will give you a straight quote.

Can one contractor handle the dirt work, concrete and framing?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction clears and levels the site with equipment, forms and pours the concrete, and frames the structure. Using one outfit for all three cuts down on scheduling gaps between crews on rural jobs in Wolfforth, Lubbock and surrounding towns.

How much does a concrete pad cost?

It depends on the pad size and thickness, the site prep and leveling needed, the reinforcement, how a concrete truck reaches the spot and the finish you want. Cross Buck Construction scopes prep, forming and the pour together; send the size and photos and call (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.
