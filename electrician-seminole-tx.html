Electrical services in Seminole and Gaines County

Shops, barns and equipment buildings

Around Seminole a lot of places have a metal shop or barn that needs real power: circuits for a welder or compressor, lighting you can work under, and outlets where the tools are. We run the feeder from the meter or house, size it for the load and wire the building inside.

Well pumps and irrigation circuits

Most farms and rural homes around Seminole depend on well water. We wire well pump circuits, disconnects and controls, and troubleshoot pumps that short-cycle, trip or will not start. More on barn and well pump wiring.

Panel and service upgrades

New homes and additions around Seminole often outgrow the panel quickly, especially with a shop, AC upgrade or EV charger. We check what you have and what you are adding, then tell you straight whether you need more circuits or a bigger service.

Repairs and troubleshooting

Breakers that keep tripping, outlets that quit, lights that flicker. We find the fault and fix it, and we tell you what we found.

House wiring and remodels

Added circuits, fixtures, outlets and GFCI protection for remodels, additions and garages.

Generator hookups and EV chargers

We install transfer switches and generator inlets so a generator can keep the well and the essentials running in an outage, and Level 2 EV chargers on a properly sized 240-volt circuit.
