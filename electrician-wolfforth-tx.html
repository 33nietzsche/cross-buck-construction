Electrical work in Wolfforth

Panel and service upgrades

A full panel, breakers that trip when the AC kicks on, or new load from a shop, a welder or an EV charger are the usual reasons to look at your panel. We check what you have and what you plan to add, then tell you what the job really needs.

Level 2 EV chargers

A Level 2 charger runs on a 240-volt circuit. We check your panel capacity first, so you know whether a new circuit is enough or a service upgrade comes first, before you buy anything.
