Frequently asked questions

Do you build cattle corrals?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds working corrals, pens, alleys and basic handling layouts for ranches in West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. We lay out the pens around how you actually work cattle, so animals move through without fighting the setup. Call or text (806) 496-6999 with your town.

Can you repair or add on to an existing corral?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction repairs leaning posts, broken panels and sagging gates, and adds pens or alleys to corrals that already work for you. Because we weld, many repairs are done in place instead of tearing out the whole setup. Send photos and your town for a straight answer.

What do you need to plan a corral?

Tell us the town, roughly how many head you work, how you sort and load, where water and gates need to go, and send photos of the spot. Cross Buck Construction uses that to suggest a layout and quote materials and labor before any posts go in the ground.

Can you add water to the pens?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction also handles ranch plumbing, water lines and livestock troughs, plus the trenching to run a line. We plan the trough and line with the corral so water sits where the cattle need it, instead of being added as an afterthought once the pens are built.

Which towns do you build corrals in?
