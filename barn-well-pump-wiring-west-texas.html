Diagnosing a pump that trips or won't start

The usual causes are a failing motor, a bad pressure switch, damaged wire, water in a connection or a circuit that is too small for the pump. If a pump box or disconnect is hot, buzzing or burnt-smelling, shut it off at the breaker if you safely can, stay clear and call (806) 496-6999.

Frequently asked questions

Can you run power to a barn or shop?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction runs underground or overhead power from your meter or house to barns, shops and outbuildings, sized for the load you plan to use. We can trench the line with our own equipment, then set a subpanel and wire the circuits, lighting and outlets inside the building.

Do you wire and repair well pumps?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction wires new well pump circuits, disconnects and controls, and repairs pumps that trip, short-cycle or will not start. We work on ranch, farm and rural home wells across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico from our Wolfforth base. Call or text (806) 496-6999.

How much does it cost to wire a barn or shop?

It depends on the distance from the meter or house, trench length and soil, wire size for the load, how many circuits, lights and outlets you need, and whether your panel needs an upgrade. Cross Buck Construction scopes it from photos or a visit; call (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.

Why does my well pump keep tripping the breaker?

A well pump that trips its breaker can point to a failing pump motor, a bad pressure switch, damaged wire, water in a connection or an undersized circuit. Turn it off, stay away from wet connections, and call or text Cross Buck Construction at (806) 496-6999 to diagnose it safely.
