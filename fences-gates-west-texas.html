Gates

Pipe gates and decorative entry gates, hung to swing and latch true. Gates are built in our welding work.

Why the braces matter

A fence line is only as tight as its corners. H-braces and X-braces at corners and gates take the pull of the wire so posts don't lean and the line doesn't sag. That X-brace, the cross buck, is where Cross Buck Construction gets its name.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build new ranch fence?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds new fence lines for ranches, farms and rural homes across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico, with materials matched to the ground and the job. We set corner and brace posts true and pull the line tight. Call or text (806) 496-6999 with your town.

Can you repair an old fence instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Cross Buck Construction repairs washouts, leaning posts, broken braces and loose wire, and tells you straight when a stretch is too far gone and needs a rebuild. Send photos of the line and corners to (806) 496-6999 so we can scope repair versus rebuild before quoting.

Do you install gates?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds and hangs pipe gates and decorative metal entry gates, and fixes gates that sag or drag. Because we also weld, we can build the gate, set the posts and hang it on the same job, so it swings and latches true from day one.

How much does fence cost per foot?

It depends on the fence type, post spacing and post material, the ground (rock, caliche or sand), the number of corners, braces and gates, and access along the line. Cross Buck Construction does not publish a flat per-foot price; send the rough length and photos and call (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.
