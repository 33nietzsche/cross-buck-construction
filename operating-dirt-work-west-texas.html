Frequently asked questions

What is dig and dump?

Dig and dump means we dig out material such as dirt, rock or debris with equipment, load it, and haul it off to a dump site. Cross Buck Construction scopes access, haul distance and dump fees up front, so the quote shows the real cost before any digging starts.

Do you build caliche roads and pads?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds and repairs caliche roads, driveways and pads for ranches, shops and rural homes across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. We look at the length, drainage and traffic the road has to carry, then quote materials, equipment time and hauling before work starts.

Can you trench for water lines or pipeline?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction digs trenches for water lines and pipeline installs with an excavator, then backfills once the line is set. Because we also do ranch plumbing, one outfit can trench, lay the line and connect troughs on the same job in Wolfforth, Lubbock and surrounding towns.

Do you clear brush and land?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction clears brush, small trees and debris to open paths, fence lines and building sites, then hauls off what needs to go. Send photos and the rough size of the area to (806) 496-6999 for a straight answer on equipment, time and cost.

Where do you do dirt work?

Cross Buck Construction runs equipment from its Wolfforth, Texas base to Lubbock, Brownfield, Lamesa, Midland, Odessa, Big Spring, Snyder, Sweetwater, Amarillo and Muleshoe, Texas, and Hobbs, New Mexico. Tell us the town and how a machine gets in, and we will give you a straight yes or no.

How much does a caliche driveway cost?

It depends on the length and width, how much base the ground needs, drainage work, the haul distance for caliche and equipment access. Cross Buck Construction measures and scopes those before quoting, so there are no mystery line items. Send the town and photos and call (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.
