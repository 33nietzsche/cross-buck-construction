Electrical work in Lamesa and Dawson County

Panel and service upgrades

Plenty of Lamesa houses were built with panels sized for a different era. If breakers trip when the AC and dryer run together, the panel is out of spaces, or you are adding a shop or EV charger, we look at the panel, the meter base and the load before we say what the job needs.

Repairs and troubleshooting

Dead outlets, flickering lights, a breaker that will not reset or a switch that runs warm. We track down the cause instead of swapping parts until something works. If something is sparking or smells hot, stay clear of it and call.

Wiring for houses, additions and remodels

New circuits, lighting, outlets and GFCI protection for kitchens, additions, garages and remodels in town.

Irrigation and well pumps, barns and shops

Out on the cotton farms around Lamesa, a lot of the work is well pump circuits, pump controls and power to barns, shops and equipment sheds. We size the feeder for the load and wire the building inside. See barn and well pump wiring.

EV chargers

Level 2 chargers run on a 240-volt circuit. We check your panel capacity first, so you know whether a new circuit is enough or an upgrade comes first.

Generator hookups

We install generator inlets and transfer switches so a portable or standby generator can run the well, the fridge and the lights during an outage without back-feeding the utility line.
