Inquiry Privacy Notice

Cross Buck Construction · Updated September 19, 2026

Information you provide

When you request an estimate or contact Cross Buck Construction, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, town, job details and any photos you choose to send. A Nextdoor lead form shares the information you submit with Cross Buck Construction.

Purpose of the inquiry

Your inquiry information is used to respond to your request, discuss and prepare an estimate, and coordinate the services you request. Please provide only information relevant to the job; do not submit payment-card details or sensitive identification documents through an inquiry form.

Website and service providers

The contact form on this website opens your email application to send your request. Messages and lead submissions are processed through the services used to deliver them, including email providers and Nextdoor. Those services apply their own privacy policies. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts and images from external providers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when content loads.

Questions and requests

To ask about information you have provided, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to stop following up on an inquiry, email crossbuckconstruction@gmail.com or call 806-496-6999. Identify the inquiry so we can locate it. Some records may need to be retained for ongoing work, business records or legal obligations.
