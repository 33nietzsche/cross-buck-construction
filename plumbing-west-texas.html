Frequently asked questions

Do you install livestock water troughs?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction installs livestock water troughs and the water lines that feed them, and fixes floats, valves and leaks on troughs already in place. We serve ranches and farms across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico from our Wolfforth base. Call or text (806) 496-6999 with your town.

Can you run a water line from my well to a trough or shop?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction trenches with equipment, lays the water line between your well, tank, trough, shop or house, and backfills when it is done. Doing the dirt work and the plumbing ourselves keeps it to one outfit and one schedule on rural places.

How much does it cost to run a water line to a trough?

It depends on the distance, the pipe size and type, the ground the trench crosses, the trough and float you choose, and what the line ties into at the well or tank. Cross Buck Construction trenches and plumbs the line itself; send the layout and photos and call (806) 496-6999 for a free quote.

Do you do plumbing for shops and houses?

Yes. Along with ranch and farm work, Cross Buck Construction handles repairs and replacements on shop and house water lines for rural and small-town places in Wolfforth, Lubbock, Brownfield, Lamesa, Midland, Odessa and other towns across West Texas, plus Hobbs, New Mexico.

Can you set up water for a solar well pump?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction installs solar panel well setups and can also run the line and set the trough or storage tank, so the whole livestock water system comes from one outfit. Send the well location, the distance to the trough and photos to (806) 496-6999.
