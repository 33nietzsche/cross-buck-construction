Before you get started

Questions about
your remodel.

What remodeling work does Cross Buck Construction handle?

We handle room remodels and additions, including framing, flooring and tile, drywall and paint, plumbing updates, and electrical fixes and updates. We review the specific work and existing conditions before agreeing on the scope.

Can you help with a room addition?

Yes. Send the location, what you want to add and any plans you already have. We review how the addition connects to the existing building and discuss the plans, site conditions and permit requirements that apply to the project before quoting.

Can I call about a small repair or one room?

Yes. You can contact us about a single-room update, flooring, drywall, plumbing or electrical repairs as well as a larger remodel. Tell us what needs attention and send photos if you have them.

What affects the cost of a remodel or addition?

The size of the space, existing conditions, materials, layout changes, access, and plumbing or electrical work all affect the scope. Plans and permits may also be part of an addition. We review those details before pricing the work.

What should I send for a quote?

Start with your name, contact information, town and a short description of the work. Photos, rough measurements, existing plans and your preferred timing help. Use our quote form or call or text (806) 496-6999.
