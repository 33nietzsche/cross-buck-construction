About Cross Buck Construction

Anything and everything — solid jobs across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico.

Who’s behind it

I’m Zach — licensed electrician and operator by trade. 28 years in construction. I weld. I install solar panel wells and solar panel arrays on houses. I also do ranch and farm plumbing, pipeline, corrals, fences, concrete, framing, and general property work.

Cross Buck Construction can do anything and everything — solid jobs across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. You deal with the person who shows up. No call center. No mystery crew.

What “Cross Buck” means

A cross buck is that classic X-brace on ranch gates and fences — strong, simple, built for real work.

That’s the standard: electrical, solar, welding, operating, construction, plumbing, and the builds that keep a place working. We build long-term working relationships. Straightforward prices, hard work, and a job worth calling us back for.

West Texas and Eastern New Mexico, from Hobbs, NM to Sweetwater to Amarillo to Muleshoe, TX. Towns include Wolfforth, Lubbock, Brownfield, Lamesa, Midland, Odessa, Big Spring, Snyder, Sweetwater, Amarillo, Muleshoe TX, and Hobbs NM.
