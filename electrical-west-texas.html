Frequently asked questions

Do you install Level 2 EV chargers?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction installs Level 2 EV chargers for homes and shops in Wolfforth, Lubbock and across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. A Level 2 charger runs on 240 volts, so we check your panel capacity first and tell you straight whether you need a new circuit or a service upgrade.

When do I need a panel upgrade?

You may need a panel or service upgrade if breakers trip often, the panel has no open spaces, or you are adding a shop, air conditioning, a welder or an EV charger. Cross Buck Construction checks your current panel and planned load, then explains what is needed and gives a straight quote.
