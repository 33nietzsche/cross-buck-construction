Solar well pumps for ranch water

On a remote well there is often no power line to run out, so the sun does the work where the cattle are. Most jobs cover panels, mount, controller and pump, plus a tank or trough and the line between them. The full details are on our solar well pumps page.

Tank, trough and line

The water still has to get somewhere. Troughs and lines come from our plumbing work, and trenching from our dirt work, so there is no handoff between crews.

Solar panel arrays on houses

Site check

We look at the roof or ground space, shade from trees and buildings, and what you want the system to do for the house.

Straight quote

The quote spells out what is included and what is not. We don't promise savings figures or payback periods; we tell you what goes on the house and what it takes to put it there. Hookup to the house is part of our electrical work.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install solar panels on houses?
