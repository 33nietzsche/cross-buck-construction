Frequently asked questions

What welding work does Cross Buck Construction do?

Cross Buck Construction welds gates, pipe, equipment and ranch steel. That includes pipe fence and corral panels, gate builds and repairs, broken brackets and equipment fixes. Zach does the welding himself, so the person who scopes the job is the person who does the work. Call or text (806) 496-6999.

Do you build custom ranch gates?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction builds and repairs ranch gates, including pipe gates and decorative metal entry gates like the one in our jobsite photos. We set the posts, hang the gate and make sure it swings and latches true. We serve West Texas and Eastern New Mexico from our Wolfforth base.

Can you repair broken ranch equipment?

Yes, when the repair is steel work: cracked brackets, broken hitches, worn pipe and similar fixes on ranch and farm equipment. Send photos of the break to (806) 496-6999 and Cross Buck Construction will tell you straight whether it is a weld repair or a replacement part before any work starts.

Do you weld pipe fence and corral panels?

Yes. Cross Buck Construction welds pipe fence, pipe corral panels, alleys and gate frames for places in Wolfforth, Lubbock, Lamesa, Brownfield and across West Texas and Eastern New Mexico. We match pipe size and post spacing to your livestock and ground, and the quote spells out materials and labor.

How do I get a welding quote?

Text photos and your town to (806) 496-6999, or use the contact form at crossbuckconstruction.com. Tell us what broke or what you want built, rough sizes, and whether the steel is already on site. Cross Buck Construction answers with a straight yes or no and a clear quote.
